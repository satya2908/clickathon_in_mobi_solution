Chat bubble

The button sits bottom right. Opening it mounts LibreChat in an iframe, which in turn reaches ClickHouse through the MCP server.

What working looks like

  • The panel opens and shows LibreChat rather than a blank box.
  • Signing in once survives a reload — if it does not, the page is being served from file:// and the session cookie is being dropped.
  • A conversation offers the verdict-clickhouse tools.
  • Asking for the fill rate of os_version=Android 15 between 23 and 26 June 2026 returns 0.43331. Any other number means the model answered from memory.

The last one is the only check that proves anything. The rest can pass while the model is inventing.
